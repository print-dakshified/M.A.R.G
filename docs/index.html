01 / The problem

Simple tasks shouldn't feel complicated.

For many people, applying for a government service begins with a form they don't fully understand. What should be a straightforward process can become confusing, stressful, and time-consuming.

Language barriers

Official instructions may not be available in a language people are comfortable with.

Complicated terminology

Unfamiliar government terms make even basic questions difficult to answer.

Fear of making mistakes

Uncertainty about what to enter can lead to incorrect details and repeated applications.

Lack of guidance

Not everyone has access to someone who can explain the process when they need help.

02 / Our purpose
We believe technology should make essential services easier to reach, not harder to understand.

Why M.A.R.G?

M.A.R.G was created with a simple thought: people should not struggle to access services simply because a form is difficult to understand.

We want to bridge the gap between complicated official information and the people who need it.

Through simple explanations and multilingual assistance, M.A.R.G aims to make digital government services more approachable.

03 / What we do

Guidance when you need it.
